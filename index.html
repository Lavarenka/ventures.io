What holds it together

The fractures aren't damage.
They're the structure.

Every vein of light across the stone traces a real fault line — generated procedurally, no two views ever land on quite the same pattern. What looks broken is what's actually load-bearing.

The surface itself is a single continuous form, deformed and lit in real time, entirely in your browser.

What it's suspended above

A sea with no shoreline,
only depth.

Layered swells, chop, and fine ripple move independently across the surface — lit from a single low sun that never quite sets. Watch long enough and the horizon stops looking flat.
